Wangs - Korean Convenience Store Website

Wangs is an e-commerce website that brings the authentic flavors of Korea to your doorstep. The website is inspired by the physical store Wang, located at Lapu-Lapu Airport Rd, MEPZ 1, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu. To emphasize its connection to the original store and give it a distinct identity online, we added an "s" to the name, transforming it into Wangs.

This platform offers a variety of Korean and Japanese products, including noodles, kimbap, and kimchi. With detailed sections for each product category, customers can easily explore their favorite items. The website is designed to make shopping convenient, while staying true to the authentic charm of the original store.

Whether you're craving the spice of SHIN Ramyun or the tang of fresh kimchi, Wangs combines online convenience with the trust and authenticity of its physical location.
