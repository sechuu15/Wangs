Store Policy

1. Refund Policy

  • We will refund or exchange items when they are:
    • Damaged in packaging or the product itself.
    • Discordant with the customer’s original order.
  • Procedure for Refund:
    • Show us evidence of the damaged item with its receipt through our store cellphone or Messenger.
    • Return the damaged item with the receipt within 7 working days – shipping charges will be covered by Wangs.
    • Refund to customer’s bank account or exchange the item.
  • Items purchased from Wangs.com are not eligible for return, exchange, or refund for any other reasons.

2. Delivery Rules

  • Free Delivery:
    • Area 1: Minimum order PHP 1,500 (Delivery charge PHP 150 for orders below the minimum).
    • Area 2: Minimum order PHP 3,000 (Delivery charge PHP 250 for orders below the minimum).
    • Province 1: Minimum order PHP 5,000 (Delivery charge PHP 500 for orders below the minimum).
    • Province 2: Minimum order PHP 10,000 (Delivery charge PHP 1,000 for orders below the minimum).

3. Personal Information

  • The store will ask for basic information for better service, only with customer consent:
    • Name or nickname.
    • Bank account details (if needed for refund).
    • Cellphone number and address for delivery.
    • User name and email address for signing up on our site.
  • Personal information will not be used for any purposes other than purchasing and refunding.

4. Payment Methods

  • G-Cash
  • PayPal
  • Bank Transfer (BPI)
  • Cash on Delivery (Area 1 Only)
